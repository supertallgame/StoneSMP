KEY COMMANDS

Essential in-game commands to help you get started.

/withdraw [amount]

Convert your extra hearts into physical items.

/clan create [name]

Create a clan and invite your teammates.

/tpa [player]

Request teleportation to another player.

/spawn

Teleport safely back to the central spawn hub.
