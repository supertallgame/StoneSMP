Fair Play

Server Guidelines

1

Max Team Size Limit (5 Players)

Teams and clans are strictly capped at a maximum of 5 members. Cross-teaming beyond 5 players during PvP fights or events is prohibited.

2

Respect World Border Limits

All gameplay occurs within the 7.5k x 7.5k map boundary. Attempting to bypass or glitch outside the border will result in punishment.

3

No Unfair Advantage or Hacking

Hacked clients, x-ray, auto-clickers, macro scripts, or exploit abuse are strictly banned. Use /report to notify staff of violators.

4

Community Chat & Conduct

Maintain basic respect in global chat. Severe harassment, hate speech, or excessive spam will result in muted chat privileges or temporary bans.
